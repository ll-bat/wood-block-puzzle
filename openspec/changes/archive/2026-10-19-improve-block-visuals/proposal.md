# Improve block visuals

## Why

The game mechanics and Challenge workflow are complete, but the flat board cells and single falling clear effect do not provide the tactile, satisfying presentation expected from a modern casual block puzzle.

## What Changes

- Add coordinated, persistent cube and board palettes selectable from Challenge Mode.
- Render recessed board wells, dimensional cubes, and a rounded board frame without external image dependencies.
- Replace the single clear motion with staggered burst, flip, and spark effects while keeping board state authoritative.
